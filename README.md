## Theoretical Foundation

### The Ontological Break from Scaling Laws

Contemporary AI development operates under the "Scaling Laws" hypothesis: that linear increases in data and compute power inevitably lead to general intelligence. The Draken framework identifies this paradigm as approaching thermodynamic and informational asymptotes. The alternative proposed here is not faster silicon—it is a fundamental restructuring of how intelligence is *defined*.

Intelligence in the Draken ontology is a **physical phenomenon**: energy flowing through constraints. This aligns with and extends the **Maximum Entropy Production Principle (MEPP)**, which states that non-equilibrium thermodynamic systems organize to maximize entropy production (dissipation) for degrading energy gradients. Life itself is the exemplar of this principle.

### Assembled Complexity vs. Strong Emergence

Unlike reductionist physicalism (explaining complex phenomena through constituent parts alone) or strong emergence (positing inexplicable novelties), **Assembled Complexity** describes emergent properties at higher layers as *causally constrained by lower-level physical laws while possessing downward causal power*. A social norm (Layer 10) is constrained by neurobiological limits (Layer 8) but can reshape the metabolic patterns of entire populations. This bidirectional causality—upward constitution and downward governance—is the architectural principle that enables Draken's Planetary Feedback Loop.

## Mathematical Formalism

### 2.1 Relational Meaning via Sheaf Theory

In classical AI, meaning is typically defined through statistical co-occurrence of tokens—vector proximity in a latent space. Draken formalizes meaning topologically.

**Definition (Relational Meaning):** Let $\mathcal{X}$ be the topological space representing the Assembled Complexity of Earth. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a sheaf on $\mathcal{X}$ representing semantic value. The *meaning* $\mu$ of an entity $x \in \mathcal{L}_k$ (residing in Layer $k$) is defined as the set of its partial derivatives with respect to all other layers:

$$\mu(x) = \left\{ \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_j}{\partial x} \;\middle|\; j \neq k \right\}$$

This asserts that a gene sequence in Layer 7 carries no absolute meaning. Its meaning is the vector of:
- $\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{organism}} / \partial x$: the phenotypic expression
- $\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{ecosystem}} / \partial x$: impact on local biodiversity
- $\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{civilization}} / \partial x$: implication for agricultural stability or biosecurity

A *global section* of $\mathcal{F}$ that is consistent across all layers constitutes a valid, grounded meaning. Contradictions between local sections surface as **sheaf cohomology** — measurable as a "consistency radius" via the `PySheaf` library.

The **Persistence Barcode** of global conversation allows Draken to detect when a narrative is becoming brittle (fragmentation into disconnected $H_0$ components) or rigid (domination by a single tight $H_1$ loop), enabling pre-emptive social entropy mitigation.
